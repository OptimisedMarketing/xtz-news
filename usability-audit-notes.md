# XTZ.News Usability Audit Notes

## Homepage reader journey

- The homepage exposes several competing information layers above the editorial content: a breaking-news strip, a token ticker, utility links, the main navigation, a hero carousel, secondary live-network data, and category chips. The breadth is useful to experienced Tezos readers but creates a high initial cognitive load for new visitors.
- Navigation exposes many top-level options, while the search field is visually secondary to the page hierarchy.
- The homepage includes multiple conversion paths, including donation and newsletter subscription, but the editorial value proposition and a clear next reading action could be more prominent.

## Article reader journey

- Article pages have strong foundations: a full-width featured image, clear title and metadata, a dedicated Key Takeaways block, audio controls, text-size controls, light-reading mode, share controls, tags, sources, and related content.
- The reading interface presents many utilities near the article start; these can compete with the headline and initial reading task, particularly on smaller screens.
- Key Takeaways are valuable but individual bullets can be long and should be optimised for rapid scanning.
- A reader is given several ways to continue, but related-content recommendations would benefit from stronger context and a clearer next-step hierarchy.

## Archive and international-reader journey

- The Latest News archive provides a useful in-context search field and clear newest, oldest, and popular sorting controls. It has a strong card grid, but article cards rely on small supporting metadata and could expose more skim-friendly signals such as reading time and a concise summary.
- The published Chinese article route currently shows an "Invalid language code" page and an undefined page title instead of translated content. This is a high-priority broken reader journey because the language selector suggests that international articles are available.
