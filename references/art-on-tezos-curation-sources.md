# Art on Tezos Curation Sources

## Curated project-profile basis

The three new directory profiles use only their own public pages or established first-party Tezos editorial material. They are grouped in the hub as **creator tools** or **exhibitions**. The existing Objkt.com, Teia, and fxhash profiles remain the marketplace entries in the new reader-facing filter.

| Profile | Hub group | Source-grounded description | Primary source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EditArt | Creator tools | A Tezos generative-art platform where a visitor can co-create a piece using five sliders; its site also presents series and marketplace routes. | [EditArt](https://www.editart.xyz/) |
| InfiniteInk | Creator tools | A platform that states it lets creators build, deploy, and own custom Tezos smart contracts, including open and limited editions. | [InfiniteInk](https://infiniteink.art/) |
| HEK × Tezos Digital Art Programme | Exhibitions | A 2026 HEK Basel and Tezos Foundation partnership focused on presentation, education, and preservation of blockchain-based art. The earlier 404_LAND exhibition ran from 12 June to 9 August 2026, so the profile does not describe it as currently open. | [Tezos Spotlight](https://spotlight.tezos.com/inside-the-hek-tezos-foundation-partnership/) |

## Editorial-submission design

The embedded form is intentionally limited to project information needed for editorial review: submitter name and email, submitter type, project name, website, collection group, and a concise description. A submission is stored as `pending` and is not published automatically. The form makes no promise of placement, coverage, or response time, and requires the submitter to confirm that the supplied information is accurate and that they may submit it for review.
