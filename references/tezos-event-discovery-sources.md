# Weekly Tezos Event Discovery — Approved Sources

The cloud worker is limited to the two publicly reachable calendars below. It uses one ordinary HTTP GET per source during a weekly run, with an identifying user agent, timeout, response-size cap, and no login, proxy, browser impersonation, CAPTCHA handling, or alternate endpoint.

| Source key | Public calendar | Candidate scope | Review rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `thetezoscommunity` | [TheTezosCommunity events](https://thetezos.com/event-category/community-events/) | Future community events listed on the published calendar | Imported as a pending editorial candidate only. |
| `art-on-tezos-luma` | [Art on Tezos calendar](https://luma.com/art-on-tezos?k=c) | Future events made public by the Art on Tezos calendar | Imported as a pending editorial candidate only. |

Candidates are deduplicated by source and source event URL. A candidate is never visible to readers until an XTZ.News administrator chooses **Publish to Events**. The cloud worker cannot publish, alter, approve, or reject a candidate.
