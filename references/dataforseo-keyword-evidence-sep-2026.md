## Data-Quality Findings

The US keyword-suggestion calls for `tezos wallet` and `tezos staking` returned no suggestion items under the measured-volume filter. The Etherlink suggestion set was heavily contaminated by unrelated **3Com EtherLink** hardware queries. Those legacy hardware terms are excluded from all editorial, metadata, and internal-linking decisions.

The bounded seed calls did not return usable records for `tezos defi`, `tezos smart rollups`, `KT1 contract`, `tz1 address`, `tz5 wallet`, or `tezos post quantum`. This is a reason to retain these as topical depth and news-supporting pages—not to manufacture search-volume claims. The post-quantum hub and tz5 guide remain valuable reader resources, but will not be represented as high-volume opportunities without qualifying DataForSEO evidence.

## Immediate Evidence-Backed Priorities

## XTZ.News Ranking and Live SERP Findings

DataForSEO returned one measurable top-20 US organic ranking for `xtz.news`: the existing `/explorer` page ranks **16th** for `tzkt` with a title of **Tezos Blockchain Explorer**. The query has 30 Google Ads searches and 51 clickstream searches in the retrieved data, but a difficulty signal of 80. The appropriate immediate action is not to target the third-party brand as the main keyword; it is to improve the current Explorer page’s crawlable description and its links to the on-site wallet/address learning path.

The live US Google SERP for `tezos wallet` is dominated by first-party wallet and established brand pages. Its People Also Ask results are largely price-forecast and investment-sentiment questions, which XTZ.News should not answer as predictions. The result does, however, confirm reader demand for a **safe, educational Tezos wallet starting point**. The on-site response should therefore be a neutral guidance route connected to the existing Wallet Inspector and prefix glossary, not a wallet recommendation or competitor-comparison page.

The broad keyword-ideas call was also contaminated by generic wallet brands such as Phantom, Trust Wallet, Exodus, MetaMask, Ledger, and non-Tezos terms. These are deliberately excluded from the content plan. Relevance is a mandatory selection criterion, not merely search volume.
