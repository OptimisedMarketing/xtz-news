# Teztree Community Spotlight — Verified Research

## Official Teztree site

- Teztree describes itself as a Tezos-focused link-in-bio page where a person claims a handle through a Tezos wallet, gathers links, shows minted work, and accepts tez tips.
- Authentication is wallet based: a user signs a message rather than creating an email-and-password account.
- Profiles can feature an item for sale using its live objkt price or display a self-updating grid of recent mints.
- Visitors may send tez from a profile page. Teztree says creators set preset amounts and that it does not take a cut.
- Tezos Domains can appear as a badge; the claim flow offers a matching handle when available.
- Supported ecosystem links include objkt, fxhash, Teia, Versum, Rodeo, SuperRare, Foundation and OpenSea, alongside ordinary social links.
- Profile customisation includes theme presets/custom colours, banners, button styles, shapes and typefaces. The site also offers aggregated views, link clicks and click rate, stating that events are counted once per visitor session without personal data storage.
- The service is free except handles of four characters or fewer, which cost 11.11 tez once and are verified on chain.

## Official Tezos Community Spotlight announcement

- The Tezos account announced Teztree as a Community Spotlight on 7 August 2026 and identifies @flexasaurusrex as its builder.
- The announcement characterises Teztree as a single custom page for a Tezos user's on-chain presence, links, latest mints and tez tips.
