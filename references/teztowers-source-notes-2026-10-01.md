# TezTowers Source Notes

**Researched:** 1 October 2026

## Primary project source

- [TezTowers public site](https://teztowers.com/) titles itself **“TezTowers — The Living 3D City & Virtual Real Estate on Tezos.”**
- Its public meta description says visitors can claim sequential Main St parcels, watch dynamic 3D NFTs evolve through upgrades, and launch self-serve billboard advertising campaigns.
- A browser inspection showed the public experience has a searchable 3D Main St scene, plot navigation, an advertise action, a Tezos-wallet connect action, and current live occupancy/purchase controls. Those live values are dynamic and should not be reported as fixed facts without a timestamp.
- Official sources used for stored visuals:
  - `https://teztowers.com/og-image.png` — official city / T mark preview
  - `https://teztowers.com/brand/avatar-1024.png` — official T mark avatar

## Launch posts

- [1 October launch-day post](https://x.com/TezTowers/status/2105488167042515017): TezTowers said **79 plots sold** on its first day, first-day plot holders received balloons, and perks / new destinations were coming. It linked to the project site. This is a project-reported metric, not independently audited.
- [30 September “plots filling” post](https://x.com/TezTowers/status/2105352862570365315): “The plots are filling FAST! Welcome to the neighborhood Tezos!” The post includes the Main St overview visual stored from `https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HTe3H9LbAAAgvTH.jpg?name=orig`.
- [30 September open letter](https://x.com/TezTowers/status/2105168454487785496): TezTowers offered free advertising to Tezos institutions and non-profit projects, naming @TheTezos and @TeiaCommunity, and invited discussions about bespoke Main St integrations. It does not establish a partnership, endorsement, eligibility rules or commercial terms.

## Editorial guardrails

- Describe the project as a **living 3D city / virtual-real-estate project on Tezos** based on its own public proposition.
- Attribute the 79-plot figure to TezTowers and label it as a first-day claim.
- State that wallet, purchase, plot availability, upgrade and advertising details can change; direct readers to TezTowers for current terms.
- Do not state that organisations named in the free-advertising post are partners or endorse TezTowers.
